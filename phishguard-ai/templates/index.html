Phishing Threat Model

Example threat scenarios, attack vectors, and recommended mitigations
Threat Matrix Baseline

Below are example cybersecurity threat scenarios demonstrating common phishing modalities, their potential organizational impact, and practical mitigations:

Threat Scenario Description Likelihood Impact Recommended Mitigations
1. Fake Login Emails Deceptive emails spoofing legitimate corporate or personal portals to harvest usernames and passwords. High Critical Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), password managers with origin matching, and email domain verification (DMARC/SPF).
2. Suspicious Links Hyperlinks pointing to typosquatted domains, IP addresses, or URL shorteners to direct users to fraudulent sites. High High Inspecting link URLs before clicking, web-filtering extensions, and browser sandbox isolation.
3. Fake Payment Requests Social engineering messages claiming unpaid invoices or demanding urgent wire or crypto transactions. Medium Critical Out-of-band verification via official public phone numbers; strict multi-party approval for financial disbursements.
4. Account Impersonation Attackers masquerading as trusted colleagues, executives, or support technicians to extract confidential data. High High Employee security awareness training, verifying sender identities, and establishing formal communication protocols.
5. Weak Authentication Reliance on easily bypassed single-factor passwords or SMS OTPs that increase the impact of account compromise. Medium High Adopting phishing-resistant FIDO2 hardware security keys, authenticator apps, and enforcing strong password policies.

* The scenarios above illustrate example threat models for security education, not confirmed system vulnerabilities.
